# FunnelLink: The Elite Decision Engine for High-Ticket Commerce

## 1. The Vision: From "Browsing" to "Matching"
FunnelLink is a high-end SaaS "Decision Engine" that transforms static product catalogs into high-converting, tag-aware matching journeys. We replace the primitive "Add to Cart" grid with an elite, story-driven experience designed to capture high-intent leads for high-ticket industries (Luxury Furniture, Healthcare, Jewelry, Estates).

**The Ultimate Goal:** A "Million-Dollar" SaaS that delivers a 10x ROI for business owners by providing a zero-configuration platform that turns intent into WhatsApp enquiries in 60 seconds.

---

## 2. The Core Innovation: The "Logic-First" Engine
Traditional funnels are complex "if/then" trees that take hours to build. FunnelLink introduces **Tag-Based Matching Intelligence**:
- **Automatic Matching:** Business owners tag products (e.g., `LUXURY`, `MINIMAL`). They tag question options with the same IDs.
- **Dynamic Scoring:** The engine doesn't just filter; it calculates a "Match Score" (0-100%) for every product based on user intent.
- **Zero-Friction Builder:** No complex branching logic. If tags match, products appear.

---

## 3. What We Are Actually Creating (The 3 Pillars)

### I. The "Studio" Builder (Owner Experience)
- **60-Second Fast-Track:** A "Pick & Publish" workflow where owners select a blueprint, bulk-paste their product names/prices, and go live instantly.
- **Visual Logic Command:** A single-pane editor with a live mobile preview. Edit branding on the left, see the result on the phone mockup on the right.
- **Artifact Registry:** A central database for high-ticket items, allowing owners to manage inventory once and deploy across multiple funnels.

### II. The "Discovery Journey" (Customer Experience)
- **Cinematic Flow:** A fluid, app-like interface built with Framer Motion, featuring smooth transitions between Hero, Quiz, and Results.
- **Premium Aesthetics:** A "Million-Dollar UI" standard—dark-first modes, glassmorphism, high-impact typography (Outfit/Inter), and vibrant accent palettes.
- **Match-Score Cards:** Recommendations aren't just shown; they are "Justified" with Match Scores, creating psychological trust.

### III. The "Lead Intelligence" (Conversion Engine)
- **WhatsApp Handshake:** Frictionless lead capture that bridges the gap between a digital discovery and a human sale.
- **Rich Data Handoff:** WhatsApp messages are pre-filled with the customer’s name, contact, and their high-scoring product matches.
- **Analytics Matrix:** A dashboard tracking conversion rates, lead velocity, and "Matching Accuracy" to help owners refine their logic.

## 4. Technical Architecture (The Conversion OS)
- **Engine Core:** Next.js 15 (App Router) + React 19 for sub-second performance.
- **The Brain:** A weighted scoring algorithm implemented in Client Runtime for zero-latency recommendations.
- **Data Layer:** Supabase (Auth, RLS, Storage) for military-grade multi-tenant data isolation.
- **Design System:** Tailwind CSS v4 + Vanilla CSS for bespoke, premium micro-interactions.

---

## 5. Operational Workflow

1. **Ingest (30s):** Owner pastes a list of products (Name - Price) into the Studio.
2. **Tag (15s):** Owner assigns a few tags (e.g., `WOOD`, `METAL`) to products and quiz options.
3. **Deploy (5s):** One-click publish to a unique `/s/slug` URL.
4. **Discover:** Customer takes the quiz; the engine calculates the "Top 3 Matches."
5. **Connect:** Customer hits WhatsApp; the owner receives a qualified lead with intent data.
